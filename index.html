Chatisode

Your group chat, the TV season

Chatisode turns an exported group chat into a season of TV: an episode guide of the chat's biggest days, kind cast awards, topics and real stats, and a story-style premiere to share with the group.

Everything is made on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account, no ads and no analytics, and the chat is never uploaded. With Apple Intelligence, episode titles and cast cards are written by the on-device model; without it, Chatisode writes Classic scripts and every feature still works.

  • Privacy policyData Not Collected: nothing leaves your device

Make your first season

  1. In your messenger, open the group and export the chat without media (WhatsApp: Group info → Export chat → Without media; Telegram Desktop: Export chat history with the format Machine-readable JSON).
  2. Share the exported .zip, .txt or result.json to Chatisode, or tap Import → Choose file… in Chatisode.
  3. Pick who you are and the period, then tap Start production. Watch the premiere as soon as you like.

No export button in your messaging app? Pick screenshots of the chat instead: Import → Screenshots. They're read on your device and never saved; the support page lists the languages they can be read in.

No chat at hand? Tap Try the sample show in the app: a fictional chat, made into a full season.
