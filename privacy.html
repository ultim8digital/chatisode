Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Data Not Collected

Chatisode processes your chat exports and screenshots only on your device. It collects no data: there is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own. The App Store privacy label says the same: Data Not Collected.

What the app reads

Chatisode reads only what you choose to open in it: a chat export (a .zip, .txt or .json file) or screenshots of a chat. It never accesses any messenger, account, contacts or other app.

Screenshots

If you choose screenshots, Chatisode reads the text in them on your device. Chatisode never saves the screenshots; only the chat text is used, under the same rules as an export. It also reads the date each screenshot was taken, to place the messages in time; location metadata isn't read.

The photo picker belongs to the system: Chatisode receives only the screenshots you pick and has no access to the rest of your photo library. The images are read in memory and released, and the temporary copies the system places in Chatisode's own storage are removed after each import (or at the next launch).

Processing on the device

Statistics, the episode guide, cast cards and the premiere are computed on your iPhone or iPad. When Apple Intelligence is available, short masked excerpts are written into episode titles and card text by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to a server, including Apple's Private Cloud Compute.

What stays on your device

Right after the import the full chat is discarded. A season keeps its statistics, its cards and their text. For the writing, short excerpts of the biggest days (with every member's name replaced by a placeholder) and a few topic samples are kept until the writing is done; afterwards only short summaries remain. These files are encrypted while the device is locked and are excluded from backups. Your settings (such as App Lock and haptics) are stored on the device.

Sharing

Cards you share are drawn as images on the device and handed to the system share sheet; only what you choose to send leaves the device. Images are removed from the app's temporary storage when the share sheet closes.

Deleting your data

Delete a season or a show from its menu in the app, or everything at once in Settings → Delete everything. Removing the app deletes all of its data.

If you reset the library, the old library, including its excerpts, stays on your device until you use Delete everything.

Purchases

Chatisode Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details or personal information. Restore Purchases asks the App Store for your existing purchase.

Age

Chatisode is intended for people aged 13 and over. Where the law requires it, the app uses Apple's age range feature, and people under 13 can't import chats.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive what you write and use it only to answer you. Please don't send chat exports or other sensitive content.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date.
