Export a chat

WhatsApp

Open the group, tap its name, then Export chat → Without media. Share the .zip (or .txt) to Yapisode, or save it to Files and tap Import → Choose file… in Yapisode.

Telegram

Use Telegram Desktop: open the chat, choose Export chat history, untick photos and videos and pick the format Machine-readable JSON. Move the result.json file (or a zip of the export folder) to your iPhone or iPad and open it in Yapisode.

The file doesn't open

Yapisode reads WhatsApp exports in every date format, and Telegram's JSON export. If a file still fails, export it again without media. Importing the same chat again later with more messages makes a new season of the same show.

Apple Intelligence and Classic scripts

On an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings), episode titles, cast cards, topics and host lines are written by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is uploaded, not even to Private Cloud Compute, and AI-written text carries the label Written with on-device AI.

Without Apple Intelligence, or while the model is still downloading, Yapisode writes rule-based Classic scripts from the chat's real stats. Every stat, card and share still works. Once Apple Intelligence is ready, the season page offers Rewrite with AI.

Names, consent and private days

  • Before you share, you can edit every text, rename anyone, show aliases or initials only, or leave someone out of the story completely.
  • Days that look personal (health, money, relationships, politics, arguments, loss) are kept private automatically: no episode is written about them.
  • Share your friends' cards with the group, not about them.

Yapisode Pro

Yapisode Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks every episode and cast card, topics, clean cards without the watermark and rewriting single cards with AI. Importing, stats, the first episode and one cast card are free. Pro works with Family Sharing.

To restore Pro on a new device, open Settings in Yapisode and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Your data

Chats are processed only on your device and Yapisode collects no data. You can lock the app with Face ID or your passcode, delete a season or a show from its menu, or delete everything in Settings → Delete everything. Details are in the privacy policy.
