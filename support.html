Make a season from screenshots

No export button in your messaging app? Take screenshots of the chat and pick them in Chatisode: tap Import → Screenshots → Choose screenshots (or Image files for screenshots saved in Files). Chatisode reads them on your iPhone or iPad, puts the messages in order, skips the repeats and shows the chat for checking before the season is made. It never saves the screenshots.

  1. Open the chat in your messaging app.
  2. Scroll to the first message you want and take a screenshot.
  3. Scroll on, keeping one message from the last screenshot in view, and take the next one.
  4. Pick up to 300 screenshots, in any order.
  5. In Check the chat, make sure your own bubbles are marked as yours, rename or merge people, fix any bubble that was read wrong and tap Looks right.

For the best result

  • Close the keyboard before you take the screenshots.
  • In group chats, keep the names above the bubbles in view.
  • Screenshots show part of a chat, so stats that need every message or exact times (such as reply speed) are hidden, and when the screenshots show no dates, episodes get approximate dates (≈) or become chapters. An export file is still the most accurate, if your app has one.
  • Want to see it first? Tap See how it works: Chatisode draws a few fictional screenshots on the device and runs the same import.

Languages screenshots can be read in

Text in screenshots is read by Apple's on-device text recognition. On iOS 26: Arabic, Cantonese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian (Bokmål and Nynorsk), Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese. iOS 27 adds Finnish, Hindi and Marathi.

Hebrew, Greek, Bengali, Tamil and other scripts can't be read yet; Chatisode says so instead of guessing. Hindi screenshots need iOS 27. The app itself, its cards and the premiere are in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese and Korean.

Export a chat

WhatsApp

Open the group, tap its name, then Export chat → Without media. Share the .zip (or .txt) to Chatisode, or save it to Files and tap Import → Choose file… in Chatisode.

Telegram

Use Telegram Desktop: open the chat, choose Export chat history, untick photos and videos and pick the format Machine-readable JSON. Move the result.json file (or a zip of the export folder) to your iPhone or iPad and open it in Chatisode.

The file doesn't open

Chatisode reads WhatsApp exports in every date format, and Telegram's JSON export. If a file still fails, export it again without media. Importing the same chat again later with more messages makes a new season of the same show.

Apple Intelligence and Classic scripts

On an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings), episode titles, cast cards, topics and host lines are written by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is uploaded, not even to Private Cloud Compute, and AI-written text carries the label Written with on-device AI.

Without Apple Intelligence, or while the model is still downloading, Chatisode writes rule-based Classic scripts from the chat's real stats. Every stat, card and share still works. Once Apple Intelligence is ready, the season page offers Rewrite with AI.

Names, consent and private days

  • Before you share, you can edit every text, rename anyone, show aliases or initials only, or leave someone out of the story completely.
  • Days that look personal (health, money, relationships, politics, arguments, loss) are kept private automatically: no episode is written about them.
  • Share your friends' cards with the group, not about them.

Chatisode Pro

Chatisode Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks every episode and cast card, topics, clean cards without the watermark and rewriting single cards with AI. Importing, stats, the first episode and one cast card are free. Pro works with Family Sharing.

To restore Pro on a new device, open Settings in Chatisode and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Your data

Chats and screenshots are processed only on your device and Chatisode collects no data. You can lock the app with Face ID or your passcode, delete a season or a show from its menu, or delete everything in Settings → Delete everything. Details are in the privacy policy.
